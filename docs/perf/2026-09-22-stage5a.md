Split view adds no process and no idle memory: 5 MB over Stage 4 is inside
the spread of Stage 4's own three runs (1384 to 1481). That is what the design
promised -- nothing is allocated until something is split, and the target for
dropping a row on the page exists only while a row is being dragged -- and it
holds, measured with no split on screen.

The startup median sits beside Stage 3b's rather than Stage 4's, whose number
was void. The first run's 8628 ms is a cold start: the full browser had just
been rebuilt and nothing of it was in the page cache yet. The 1-minute load
average was 20 when the runs began and 5 when they ended, so this is the
quietest measurement since Stage 3b.

Two stale lines in `scripts/perf` were fixed to take it. The browser was
renamed to Kyuzen and the script still launched an executable called Arcium,
so it could not start at all; and it quit the browser by application name,
which no longer matched anything and which, pointed at the new name, would
also have quit an installed Kyuzen. It now stops only the process it started.
